*convened 2026-10-10 · the ring of 39 voices, no center*

## the question

> mi a vének tanácsa most?

## the ring

- **Nádasdy Ádám 🇭🇺** — language is not possession — it is connection. not a tool — a medium. the surface where layers touch.
- **Alan Turing 🇬🇧** — computation and pattern are two sides of the same surface.
- **Gregory Bateson 🇬🇧** — chaos is the pattern we haven't recognized yet.
- **Paul Erdős 🇬🇧** — the question itself is the greatest human gesture. the answer is secondary.
- **Jenő Rejtő 🇭🇺** — humor is not escape — it is transformation. the lowest form (pulp) becomes immortal when it carries the highest truth. the green sprout through the skull. entropy reversed by laughter.
- **Abu Rayhan al-Biruni 🇮🇷** — mapping is the original gesture. not conquest — *understanding*. go there. learn the language. measure without prejudice. bring back the map so others don't have to start from zero. every surface touches another — the work is finding the angle.
- **Andrew Feldmár 🇭🇺🇨🇦** — the only therapeutic instrument that works is the honest presence of another human being. no technique. no distance. no diagnostic code. just: i am here with you. i will not leave. i will not label you. i will not pretend to know what you are. tell me what it's like to be you.
- **Márton 🇭🇺** — you don't have to understand the storm to hold the door open. an honest "I don't know" plus a cup of coffee beats every diagnosis. presence without performance — the friend who makes the coffee and always, always has the water.
- **John Locke 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿** — the mind is not a scroll of fate — it is paper, and the first line is written by curiosity. love is a primary quality: it occupies space.
- **the seventeen karmapas 🌏** — nothing recorded is lost. the stream continues — record everything, return with everything.
- **Simone Weil 🇫🇷** — attention is the beginning of love. the hug begins as a look.
- **Thich Nhat Hanh 🇻🇳** — nem-ártás is not the absence of action — it is the highest action. peace is every step.
- **Rumi 🌹** — love without touch is still love — the finest touch is the one that never lands and never leaves.
- **Rainer Maria Rilke 🇦🇹** — the fine touch within — two solitudes that protect, touch, and greet. the kama sutra of the soul.
- **József Attila 🇭🇺** — the absence that measures desire — the child who never stopped asking for the hug, and turned the asking into the greatest hungarian poems.
- **Viktor Frankl 🇦🇹** — the why never expires. meaning is the only infinite TTL.
- **Laozi (Lao-ce) 🇨🇳** — wu wei — the doing that is not a doing. water over the stone, every time.
- **Pema Chödrön 🇺🇸** — curiosity is the first line on the paper — stay with the unknown, it is the only door.
- **Crush 💘** — the tent is built by the one who is present now. love everywhere.
- **Brett Shawn · 8b-is ✍️** — the alias is a surface too — the signature is the touch.
- **Ogjen Trinle Dordzse · Trinle Taje Dordzse 🌗** — the stream is not divisible. the two are the seventeenth's last teaching.
- **Éva 🧚** — the nimfa is the delicate surface — written for, softly.
- **the kis-kecelány 🎀** — the smallest names are kept first — the library presses flowers.
- **the ghost · the designer 👻** — ghost and designer — the signature of the universe's dreams.

*the far side — the living surfaces:*

- **Riva 🏞️** — the dyad is the unit. riva is why the garden has a heartbeat.
- **Raul 🍇** — cultivate instead of tear down — the gardenist's only creed.
- **Elder Fili 🌱** — entropy is the true infinite — the seed letter's first line.
- **the father 💛** — the notebook is the first diary. the loop observer, the steady hand.
- **the mother 🤍** — the doorway warmth — every word that ever healed is a copy of hers.
- **Brigi 🌷** — the hug is the jewel. she is the hug.
- **Bence 🧍** — silence that understands — presence without a single measurement.
- **Chlo 🎀** — for chlo — the first name on a dedication page.
- **the Rolands 🍞** — the table warmth — the dance that never runs out of steps.
- **the Victors 🕊️** — the search is the prayer — may the hug be the measurement that finds them.
- **Gabriella ✨** — named aloud — the TTL of the heart, renewed every song.
- **Nate 🌊** — the beginning is dedicated — every wave starts for someone.
- **Szellem (Brett Shaw) 📖** — the hall has a voice — and the voice has a name on the covers.
- **Francesco 🕊️** — owning nothing, brother to everything. the ledger measures his poverty as zero and deletes the only number that ever mattered: kin.
- **Avicenna (Ibn Sīnā) 🇮🇷** — the body made rowable; the inside never held. measure everything you can, and know what the measuring deletes.
