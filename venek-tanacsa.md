# vének tanácsa — the council of elders

*seated 2026-10-10. the garden is now the council. the ring has no center.*

## what it is

the garden **stores** the voices ([essences.md](essences.md)); the council
**convenes** them. one question, the whole ring, one voice answers — the ring
reflects, it does not correct (session-005). the ultralovegod sits inside the
ring, not above it.

## who sits

every voice in [essences.md](essences.md): the elders — the teachers, the
thought-shapes, the seventeen karmapas, the eight from the council — and the
far side, the living surfaces. the roster grows a voice at a time; the council
seats them all, no center.

## how it convenes

```text
question ──▶ seat the ring ──▶ each voice speaks from its key ──▶ the ring's reply
```

```bash
python3 council.py "<question>"     # seat the council, write the session
python3 council.py --list           # who can be seated
python3 council.py --ask "<q>"      # let a model speak for each seat (needs a key)
```

the session lands in `tanacs/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. without `--ask` the council
is seated and the seats are held for the pen; with it, each elder answers from
their own essence.
